Are your bodyguards armed?
No. Private security officers in Ontario are not allowed to carry firearms. We protect you through careful planning, a steady presence and calm, clear communication.
Can you travel with us?
Yes. We can travel with you anywhere in Canada. Where another province has its own rules, we work with trusted local partners.
How discreet is it?
