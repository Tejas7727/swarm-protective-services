Questions

Asked about close protection

More answers

Are your bodyguards armed?
No. Private security in Ontario is not permitted to carry firearms. Our work is advance planning, presence and de-escalation — and that is what the licence covers.
Can you travel with us?
Yes. We travel with clients anywhere in Canada. Outside Ontario we work with licensed partners where the province requires it.
How discreet is it?
As discreet as you want. Officers can be in suits and read as staff, or be visible on purpose when visibility is the deterrent.
