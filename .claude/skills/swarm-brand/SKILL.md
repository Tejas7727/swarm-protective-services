# Swarm Protective Services — brand system

Licensed door staff, event security and close protection across the Greater Toronto
Area. The audience is a venue owner, promoter or executive deciding whether to call
tonight. Every page has one job: produce a phone call or a quote request.

## Voice

Plain, specific, unsentimental. The product is that nothing happens. Never use
fear as a sales lever and never imply violence — the differentiator is licensed,
trained staff who de-escalate. Concrete beats abstract: "two guards at the door by
twenty to nine" not "rapid response times".

Never write: cutting-edge, seamless, bespoke, elevate, peace of mind, your trusted
partner, in today's world.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| coal | `#1C1F20` | base surface |
| deep | `#141617` | recessed / page background |
| gold | `#B8914E` | accent — dark backgrounds only |
| bone | `#E7E4D3` | body and headline text on dark |
| mute | `#8E8D85` | secondary text |
| ink  | `#14171A` | text and logo on white |

Hard rule: the gold logo never goes on white. On white or light backgrounds use the
`-light` logo files, which invert to black artwork with gold accents. Charcoal, never
navy — navy reads as IT and cyber security, not physical.

## Type

- Display: **Saira**, width 105–115, weight 700–800, uppercase. Squared industrial,
  matches the wordmark.
- Body: **Archivo**, 400/500.
- Do not set body copy in Saira.

## Logo files — `brand/logo/`

Naming is `swarm-[lockup]-[colourway].svg`.

Lockups: `full` (stacked, default) · `horizontal` (headers, letterhead) ·
`horizontal-compact` (nav under 40px tall) · `emblem` · `emblem-compact`
(favicon and anything under 64px — inner shield outline removed so it does not fill in) ·
`bee` (social, merch) · `swarm-only` · `wordmark`.

Colourways: `gold` (dark bg) · `light` (white bg) · `bone` (one-colour reversed) ·
`ink` (one-colour on light).

Minimum sizes: primary 110px / 30mm · horizontal 190px / 48mm · emblem 64px / 18mm ·
emblem-compact 20px / 8mm · embroidery 60mm.

Clear space on all sides equals the width of the shield's outline.

Never redraw, recolour, rotate, stretch, bevel or shadow the emblem. Place the file.
